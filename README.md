forging sides is a weapon crafting simulator where the player crafts weapons for NPCs. if you make a mistake when crafting, you are given a strike and if you get 3 strikes, the game ends. players are given 20 seconds to make the weapon requested, if the player is unable to make the weapon they lose, the player wins once they have successfully made 5 weapons (Temporary functionality for prototype).

the controls for the game are as follows:
W: Move Forward.
A: Move Left.
S: Move Backwards.
D: Move Right.
Mouse: Look
E: Interact
LMB: Grab/Drop

the parts to make weapons are as follows:

Stick
Spear Tube
Spear Tip
Steel Bar
Revolver Grip

the weapon recipes are as follows:

1 Hammer: 1 Steel Bar + 1 Stick
1 Revolver: 1 Brass Tube + 1 Revolver grip
1 Spear: 1 Spear Tip + 1 Stick
